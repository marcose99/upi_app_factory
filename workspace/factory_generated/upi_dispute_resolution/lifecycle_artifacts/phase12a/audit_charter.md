# Phase 12A Independent Audit, Assurance, and Value Validation Charter

Phase 12A establishes independent audit for both the Agentic AI factory and every generated application.

Audit principle:
- audit before generation proves factory readiness;
- audit after generation proves generated application value.

Scope:
- factory governance and repeatability
- agentic AI safety and autonomy controls
- UPI domain guardrails and guideline awareness
- architecture, HLD, LLD, and design quality
- code quality and maintainability
- test design, coverage, validation, and evaluation
- security, privacy, RAG/context, observability, audit logging
- business value and human validator usability

Boundary:
- no claim of regulatory compliance, production readiness, RBI/NPCI certification, or live payment capability.
