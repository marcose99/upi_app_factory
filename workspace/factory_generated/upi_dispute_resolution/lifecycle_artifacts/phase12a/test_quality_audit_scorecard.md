# Test Quality Audit Scorecard

Score scale:
- 0 missing
- 1 weak
- 2 partial
- 3 acceptable
- 4 strong
- 5 excellent

Required fields for each finding:
- dimension
- score
- evidence_paths
- findings
- risks
- recommendations
- decision

Pre-generation status:
- baseline criteria defined.
- evidence to be populated during and after governed generation.
