# Code of Conduct

We want technical collaboration in this repository to be respectful, evidence-based and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and defects without attacking people.
- Review code and architecture on technical evidence.
- Give reproducible examples and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy and never post secrets, real customer data or payment credentials.
- Accept fail-closed safety and governance boundaries even when they slow a change.
- Help new users understand the local/mock scope without overstating production or certification claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or personal data, destructive testing against systems you do not own, and attempts to enable real payment/provider activity through the public evaluation workflow are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, use a repository issue without exposing private personal information. For security-sensitive matters, follow `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit, hide or close contributions that violate these expectations.
