# Phase 20 — Environment Promotion Governance

Phase 20 defines a local-first enterprise promotion model for the governed autonomous factory.
It introduces promotion evidence, rollback rules, environment boundaries, and human approval gates while preserving certification-ready-not-certified posture.

The factory may prepare promotion evidence and non-production readiness decisions, but it must not automatically promote to production, mutate external systems, perform live provider calls, or claim official certification.
