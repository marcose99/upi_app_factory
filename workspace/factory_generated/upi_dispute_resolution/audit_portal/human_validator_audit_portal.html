Important Boundary

The primary UPI/payment dispute-resolution application is intended to be a real, locally runnable generated application. NPCI, RBI, banks, PSPs, ODR systems, payment rails, ledgers, notification systems, and customer systems remain mock/simulated. This portal must not claim regulatory compliance, certification, production readiness, or live payment capability.
