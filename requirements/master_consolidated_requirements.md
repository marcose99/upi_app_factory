# UPI App Factory — Master Consolidated Requirements

## Product identity

The current product name is **UPI App Factory** and its canonical identifier is
`upi_app_factory`. External conversation or workspace labels are not product
identifiers and must not appear in product source, branding, documentation,
reports, evidence, manifests, services, prompts, policies, generated content,
or handoff packages.

The repository shall be migrated through governed refactoring to the canonical
`upi_app_factory` identity. The pre-canonical repository identifier may remain
only through bounded compatibility mechanisms, immutable migration evidence,
and deferred physical or remote rename records. The generated demonstration
application remains `upi_dispute_resolution`.

## Governing posture

The product shall be local-first, deterministic-first, evidence-driven,
Linux-distribution-neutral, safety-by-design, security-by-design, and governed
by explicit human accountability. External UPI, bank, PSP, NPCI, RBI, payment
rail, and provider ecosystems remain mocked or simulated. No official
certification, regulatory approval, production readiness, live payment
integration, or real money movement shall be claimed.

## Portal-first operation

After the service is started, the complete normal workflow shall be available
from the portal: readiness, requirement submission, interpretation, assumptions,
risks, plan approval, autonomous engineering, live structured events,
application discovery, application start/stop/restart, OpenAPI discovery,
scenario testing, full validation, KPI calculation, evidence, reports, handoff,
and service management.

The CLI remains an administrative fallback for recovery, diagnostics, CI,
automation, and recipient replay.

## Bounded autonomy

One scoped run authorization shall permit all approved low-risk work without
repeated manual intervention. Destructive operations, governance or safety
weakening, real integrations, unrestricted network access, merge, tag, push,
release, remote rename, production deployment, and certification claims remain
human-controlled.

Workflows must be checkpointed and resumable. Operators shall receive
exception-oriented summaries instead of being required to monitor raw logs.

## Minimal LLM use

LLM use is disabled by default. File discovery, search, parsing, classification
rules, dependency analysis, validation, testing, arithmetic, evidence, state
transitions, process supervision, and packaging shall be deterministic.

An LLM may be used only for a small unresolved residue, with explicit
enablement, batching, caching, sanitized minimal context, strict schemas,
independent validation, and hard budgets. Normal Phase 46A execution requires
zero LLM calls.

## Portal information and user experience

The portal shall include Factory A-to-Z, Application A-to-Z, architecture,
requirements, agents, tools, prompts, policies, test laboratory, evidence,
provenance, KPIs, reports, autonomy, safety, ethics, responsibility, risk,
service management, portability, and handover.

It shall be rich but calm, accessible, progressively disclosed, keyboard
navigable, screen-reader compatible, and responsive. Up to approximately 25%
meaningful motion may be used for lifecycle, architecture, agent handoff,
traceability, test, evidence, runtime, and KPI state changes. Reduced-motion and
motion-off modes are mandatory. No simulated progress is permitted.

## KPIs and intelligence

KPIs shall be evidence-backed, formula-defined, versioned, explainable,
trendable, comparable, and auditable. Missing evidence means NOT_EVALUATED,
never green. Critical blockers override aggregate scores. Each KPI shall expose
its formula, evidence, freshness, trend, cause, impact, recommended action, and
blocking status.

## Testing and reports

The factory and generated applications shall support unit, integration,
contract, functional, negative, boundary, idempotency, duplicate, concurrency,
security, governance, mock-boundary, resilience, replay, regression,
performance, load where locally feasible, end-to-end, evidence, portability,
and recipient-replay testing.

The system shall generate distinct application, factory, combined readiness,
security, governance, safety, ethics/responsibility, risk, service-readiness,
portability, and handover reports from validated machine-readable sources.

## Linux neutrality and paths

No factory, portal, generated-application, test, script, service, report, or
handoff runtime behavior may depend on `/home/marcose`, Ubuntu, apt, systemd, a
fixed checkout name, or a fixed repository path. Paths shall come from explicit
configuration, environment variables, pathlib, XDG-compatible defaults,
temporary-directory APIs, package resources, or validated script-relative
discovery.

Platform, package-manager, and service-manager capabilities shall be detected.
Support claims require validation. Ubuntu may remain one reference environment
but is not an architectural dependency.

## Managed-service handover

Another authorized organization must be able to install, configure, start,
stop, monitor, support, back up, restore, upgrade, roll back, diagnose, audit,
and operate the complete factory as a service. Handover shall include source,
configuration, dependencies, SBOMs, reports, evidence, tests, manifests,
checksums, runbooks, service procedures, incident/change/problem procedures,
backup/restore, disaster recovery, recipient replay, and acceptance evidence.

## Phase 46A

The first implementation is a deterministic autonomous transformation
foundation. It shall verify the immutable baseline, inventory the repository,
classify findings, build a dependency-aware transformation task graph, define
validation and protected-action matrices, persist checkpoints and evidence, and
produce one concise review bundle.

Phase 46A performs no product rename, physical checkout rename, remote rename,
commit, merge, tag, push, release, or LLM call.
